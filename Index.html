🧠 What Is an Algorithm?

Story: Meet Dr. Maya, a forest scientist in Kalimantan. She studies how plants and animals live together. She can't wait years to see what happens when it rains less — so she writes an algorithm that predicts it!

An algorithm is a list of steps a computer follows, in order, to solve a problem.

Here is a simple algorithm — like a recipe:

🥞 Example Algorithm – Making a Sandwich

Step 1: Take two slices of bread 🍞

Step 2: Spread butter 🧈

Step 3: Add cheese 🧀

Step 4: Put the slices together 🥪

The computer (or the chef!) does these steps IN ORDER.

🌳 Dr. Maya's Ecosystem Algorithm

Dr. Maya wrote this algorithm to predict what happens in her forest:

1
CHECK the rain level. If rain is LOW (below 30%), the ground is dry.
2
CHECK the temperature. If temperature is HIGH (above 33°C), water evaporates faster.
3
UPDATE the plants. If there is less water, plants DECREASE.
4
UPDATE the herbivores (rabbits, deer). If plants decrease, they DECREASE too.
5
UPDATE the carnivores (tigers). If herbivores decrease, they DECREASE too.

🎯 Practice: Predict Before You Run

Question 1: If the rain becomes VERY HIGH (95%), what happens?

Question 2: If the temperature becomes very high (40°C), which comes FIRST?

Question 3: Why must an algorithm follow a SEQUENCE?
